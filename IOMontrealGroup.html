IO Montreal Group

Montreal has an active community of IO economists. I help in the coordination of the following activities.

  • The HEC Montreal IO Conference (see other sponsors in the program for each year below)

    This is a joint initiative with Rob Clark, Allan Collard-Wexler, Gautam Gowrisankaran, Mathieu Marcoux, and myself that brings six speakers each year to HEC Montreal to present frontier work in IO. This is a list of some of the previous editions. This is a by-invitation-only conference.

  • Workshop on the Applications of Industrial Organization

    This is a joint initiative with Laura Lasio, Mathieu Marcoux, Nathan Yang, and myself that brings six speakers each year to Montreal to present state-of-the-art work on a specific area of IO.


    The 2020 edition of the Workshop will take place on April 17. Final program coming soon.

  • IO Reading Group

    We meet every three weeks at one of the following universities in the city: HEC Montreal, McGill, Concordia or University of Montreal, to discuss a pre-specified paper. PhD students are more than welcome to participate.
